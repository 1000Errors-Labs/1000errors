Delivery & Returns

Delivery

Wooden pieces are made to order and posted in about 1 week, leather pieces in about 2 weeks. Printed clothing and shoes are made to order by our print partner and take longer. The delivery price and estimated time for your order are shown in your basket and at checkout.

  • UK: Royal Mail Tracked 24 or Express 24. Free on orders of £100 or more, and on all sculptures 45 cm and larger.
  • Europe, USA & Canada, Australia & NZ and the rest of the world: tracked delivery, priced by size. Import VAT or duties may be charged by your country on arrival and are paid by you.
  • Printed clothing: 9–17 working days in the UK. Shoes: 18–23 working days.

Changed your mind?

You can cancel any order within 14 days of receiving it. Email us first, then send the item back unused and in its original packaging within 14 days. Return postage is paid by you. We'll refund the item price and the standard delivery cost within 14 days of receiving it back.

This doesn't apply to custom commissions or printed clothing and shoes, which are made to order just for you, unless they arrive faulty.

Damaged or faulty?

If anything arrives damaged or develops a fault, email us within 30 days with a photo and we'll repair, replace or refund it, including postage. The LED electronics have a 12-month guarantee.
