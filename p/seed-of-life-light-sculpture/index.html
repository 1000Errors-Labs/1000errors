In essence, the Seed of Life symbol, with its seven interconnected circles, symbolizes the interconnection of life on Earth and universal existence. It also symbolizes that all life originated from a single source in a divine plan.

This piece has been meticulously designed with 100% geometric accuracy, precision cut with laser technology from multiple layers of Birch wood, and finally painted and assembled by hand.

The integrated LED lighting can be adjusted with the use of the small controller and features a 3 meter long discrete white power cable, a suitable AC adapapter will be included based on the country of purchase.

This first run of 10 pieces at 30cm are for the collectors as they are all individually numbered.
