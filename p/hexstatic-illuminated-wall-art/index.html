Hexstatic is a beautiful self illuminating wooden mandala light sculpture.

The design principles for this piece are derived from sacred geometry in particular the flower of life pattern. I created this design by starting with the flower of life pattern but then replacing the circles with hexagons and then adjusting the size of those hexagons, whilst maintaining the 6 way radial symmetry to create the overall design of each individual layer.

This piece has been meticulously designed with 100% geometric accuracy, precision cut with laser technology from multiple layers of Birch wood, and finally painted and assembled by hand.

The integrated LED lighting can be adjusted with the use of the small controller and features a 3 meter long discrete white power cable and a choice of different international AC adapters.
