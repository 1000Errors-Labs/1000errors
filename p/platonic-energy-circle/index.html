The Platonic Energy Circle is a beautiful self illuminating wooden mandala wall sculpture packed with sacred geometry symbolism.

This portal of sacred beauty, lights up with the discrete application of color changing LED lighting to create an amazing sight and energy to behold both for night and day.

The front most layer features a large mandala made from 12 intersecting circles with 2 dimensional representations of the platonic solids marking each intersection. From the center moving outward you can find a tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron followed by an icosahedron.

The second layer repeats this design and is painted silver to add an additional layer of depth which is emphasized with the use of the integrated LED lighting which highlights the silver differently from the third layer behind it.

The third layer features alternating patterns based on variants of the flower of life pattern, by changing the size of the individual circles that create this patterns as well as changing the shape of these circles to hexagons these new patterns were manifested.

This piece has been meticulously designed with 100% geometric accuracy, precision cut with laser technology from multiple layers of Birch wood, and finally painted and assembled by hand.

The integrated LED lighting can be adjusted with the use of the small controller and features a 3 meter long discrete white power cable and a choice of different international AC adapters.
