Privacy

We only collect what we need to run the shop:

  • Orders: your name, email, phone and delivery address, collected by Stripe at checkout, used to make and post your order and kept for our tax records (6 years).
  • Contact form: your name, email and message, sent to us by email through FormSubmit, used only to reply to you.
  • Mailing list (if you join): your email, stored with Mailchimp. Unsubscribe any time from any email.
  • Website stats: we use Umami, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you.

We never sell your details. To see or delete what we hold about you, email hello@1000errors.uk.
